About Brain Bee Bangladesh

Our Mission & Vision

The Story

Bangladesh's inaugural Brain Bee competition, organized by Dhulikona in collaboration with the International Brain Bee. This competition provides students across Bangladesh with the opportunity to challenge their cognitive abilities, develop critical thinking, and gain international recognition. Preliminary rounds are conducted online, with the national final held offline.

Our goal is simple: make world-class academic competition accessible to every brilliant young mind in Bangladesh.

About Us

Who We Are

About Dhulikona

Dhulikona is committed to fostering innovation and leadership through strategic initiatives and community engagement. Founded on principles of integrity, innovation, and inclusivity, we create meaningful impact through professional development and organizational excellence.

International Brain Bee

Founded in 1998 by Dr. Norbert Myslinski, the International Brain Bee inspires students worldwide to study neuroscience through a three-tiered competition system. Since its inception, it has motivated thousands of students to pursue careers in neuroscience and related fields.

Collaboration with IASC

In partnership with the International Astronomical Search Collaboration (IASC), a leading citizen science program, we bring proven methodologies in analytical thinking and scientific discovery to Brain Bee Bangladesh.
