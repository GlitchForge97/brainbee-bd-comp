About Dhulikona

Dhulikona is committed to fostering innovation and leadership through strategic initiatives and community engagement. Founded on principles of integrity, innovation, and inclusivity, we create meaningful impact through professional development and organizational excellence.

Our vision is to empower young minds to explore, discover, and excel in their chosen fields of study. Through collaborative partnerships and educational initiatives, we bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application, preparing the next generation of leaders and innovators.

International Brain Bee

Founded in 1998 by Dr. Norbert Myslinski, the International Brain Bee inspires students worldwide to study neuroscience through a three-tiered competition system. Students compete locally, then nationally, with winners advancing to the IBB World Championship.

Since its inception, the International Brain Bee program has motivated thousands of students to pursue careers in neuroscience and related fields, creating a global community of young scientists dedicated to understanding the brain.

The competition challenges students across three segments:

  • General Science - Comprehensive knowledge across physics, chemistry, biology, and earth sciences
  • Neuroscience - Specialized focus on brain function and cognitive science
  • IQ Challenges - Logical reasoning and pattern recognition

Partnership with IASC

In collaboration with the International Astronomical Search Collaboration (IASC), a leading citizen science program, we bring proven methodologies in analytical thinking and scientific discovery to Brain Bee Bangladesh.

IASC's expertise in engaging global volunteers with complex data analysis aligns perfectly with our mission to develop young scientific minds and foster a culture of inquiry and discovery.

This partnership enhances our competition format by incorporating rigorous proctoring standards, transparent scoring mechanisms, and international recognition platforms. Together, we create an environment where students can compete fairly while developing critical thinking skills essential for 21st-century scientific careers.
