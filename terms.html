Last Updated: February 2026 | Registration Opens: March 15, 2026

1. Eligibility

Brain Bee Bangladesh is open to all students in Bangladesh who meet the following criteria:

  • School Category: Students in Classes 9-10
  • College Category: Students in Classes 11-12, including SSC-26 and HSC-26 students

Participants must provide accurate information during registration. Any false information may result in disqualification.

2. Registration & Participation

Registration opens on March 15, 2026 and closes on April 15, 2026. The registration fee is BDT 50. Participants can register for one or more competition segments: General Science, Neuroscience, or IQ Challenges.

Each participant gets one attempt per segment. Once the exam begins, it cannot be paused or restarted.

3. Academic Integrity Policy

⚠ Important: Brain Bee Bangladesh maintains strict academic integrity standards. The following are strictly prohibited during the exam:

  • AI tools or AI-powered assistance (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.)
  • Browser extensions or plugins
  • Tab switching or opening new windows
  • External devices (mobile phones, tablets, smartwatches)
  • Communication with other participants
  • Any form of external assistance or reference materials

Violations will result in immediate disqualification from the competition. Our proctoring system automatically monitors and detects violations. Repeated or serious violations may be reported to educational institutions.

4. Exam Format & Scoring

The exam format is currently being determined and will be announced soon. More details will be provided to all registered participants via email.

  • Scoring details coming soon
  • Completion time may factor as a tiebreaker

Results will be announced in mid-March 2026 via email and our website.

5. Proctoring & Monitoring

All exams are monitored using automated proctoring systems that:

  • Monitor tab switching and browser activity
  • Detect unauthorized applications and tools
  • Record exam sessions for verification purposes

No webcam is required, but desktop monitoring is active. By registering, you consent to this monitoring for academic integrity verification.

6. Technical Requirements

Participants must use a desktop or laptop computer with:

  • Stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps recommended)
  • Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge browser
  • Proper functioning keyboard and mouse

Tablets and mobile phones are not supported. Brain Bee Bangladesh is not responsible for technical failures due to inadequate equipment or internet connection.

7. Results & Awards

Top 3 performers in each segment and category receive:

  • Crest (1st place) / Medal (2nd & 3rd place)
  • Certificate of Achievement
  • International recognition

All participants receive a Certificate of Participation. Results are final and subject to verification of academic integrity.
