Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Brain Bee Bangladesh is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website and participate in our competition.

2. Information We Collect

Registration Information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Educational institution
  • District/Location
  • Student category (School/College)
  • Competition segment selection

Exam Data:

  • Exam responses and scores
  • Exam attempt timestamps
  • System monitoring data (for academic integrity verification)

Technical Data:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Pages visited

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Processing your registration
  • Administering and proctoring exams
  • Calculating scores and rankings
  • Sending results and certificates
  • Communicating competition updates
  • Verifying academic integrity
  • Improving our competition platform

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Your registration and exam data will be retained for a period of one year from the competition date. Afterwards, data will be securely deleted unless we are required by law to retain it longer.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • International Brain Bee (for international recognition and coordination)
  • IASC partners (for collaboration purposes)
  • Legal authorities (if required by law)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us at brainbee.bd@gmail.com.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
